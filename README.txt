SAMURAI INTERACTIVE PORTAL

Open index.html locally or deploy the whole folder to Vercel/Netlify/GitHub Pages.

Important:
- Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
- Sound begins after the visitor presses “Draw the blade” because browsers require a user gesture before autoplaying audio.
- The uploaded hero video keeps its original soundtrack.

Interactions:
- Tap/click the samurai to awaken him.
- Touch/click anywhere to release embers.
- Drag across the scene to create a sword slash.
- Move the pointer to shift the world with subtle parallax.
- Use the top-right controls to mute/unmute or replay the intro.
